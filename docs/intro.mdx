# IntelliStream DataHub

<Lead>A shared model of your operation. DataHub connects the measurements your equipment
produces to the things those measurements are <em>about</em>, so a number in a report
can always be traced back to the pump, the process and the business decision it belongs
to.</Lead>

<KeyIdea>
Most organisations have plenty of data and very little **[context](/concepts/contextualization)**. A pressure reading
sits in one system, the pump it came from in another, the maintenance history in a third,
and the report that depends on all three is assembled by hand in a spreadsheet.

DataHub fixes the context problem. It stores your data alongside <span className="hl">a model of
what your operation actually is</span>, the equipment, what it does, and how it all connects,
so **questions get answered by following relationships instead of joining five systems by
hand**.
</KeyIdea>

## Why this matters now

[Industry 4.0](/start/industry-4) promises operations that sense, predict and adjust
themselves. Every one of
those capabilities depends on the same missing ingredient: <span className="t-brand">a machine-readable
model of your operation</span>. An AI agent that sees a stream of numbers with no
knowledge of which pump produced them, what that pump feeds, or what "normal" means for
that duty cycle, is guessing. Give it a [knowledge graph](/concepts/knowledge-graph) and
<span className="t-success">it reasons faster, and with far fewer wrong answers</span>.
[What AI agents make possible →](/value/ai-agents)

That model is the thing DataHub asks you to grow, one question at a time, and the thing it
is designed to make cheap to grow. Everything else, dashboards, reports, alarms, machine
learning, becomes comparatively easy once it exists.

:::tip Not sure whether this is for you?
Read [the operational data problem](/start/the-data-problem). If you recognise your own
organisation in it, the rest of this site is worth your time. If you don't, you may not
need a platform like this yet.
:::

## What DataHub gives you

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **One model over every silo** | Historians, ERP, maintenance systems and monitoring tools all reduce to the same four building blocks. You stop learning source-system schemas. |
| **Answers by traversal** | "Which assets under this process saw abnormal readings during yesterday's shift, and what was raised against them?" is one question, not a week of joins. |
| **Built for auditable numbers** | Figures come from one queryable model and reproduce. Full [lineage](/concepts/lineage-and-quality) back to raw measurements, with data-quality flags, is on the roadmap. |
| **Nothing proprietary underneath** | Open source, standard components, your infrastructure or ours. **Your model and your data leave in open formats whenever you want.** |
